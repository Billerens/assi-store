Walk into a library of your own. Two books are waiting on the display shelf, with a third on the reading table. Take one to the glass veranda, slip a bookmark between its pages and return later to the same paragraph. Favourite passages can become a small exhibition.

At the bindery, describe the tone of a story, choose a premise and title, then commission its opening chapter. Ask for another scene, a different version or a finale whenever you want. Each book keeps its own history and notes.

The workshop turns written descriptions into three-dimensional objects. Turn a model on the workbench, change its details with words and give the finished piece a place in the room. Assign the writing and modelling roles in Axiko to make new work. The three included stories, saved chapters and accepted furnishings are available offline.
