A snake crawls across an endless archipelago in 3D. The joystick on the left steers the head; a swipe turns the camera. The snake bites and spits by itself — your job is to lead it, dash through danger, whip its tail and close a ring of its body around foes.

Beams of light far away are challenges: outposts, rifts, nests, lairs of their masters, ruins, plates and mazes. Take them or pass by — they pay in relics. Three different relics with one mark give a set bonus, and some pairs grow into an evolution. Shards stay after a run and buy upgrades that last.

The game speaks English and Russian, following the language of the app.
