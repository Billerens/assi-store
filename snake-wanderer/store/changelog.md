# Snake Wanderer: what's new

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-19

- The game is in the store: a snake on an endless 3D archipelago, challenges to take or leave, a hundred relics that combine into builds and evolutions, lasting upgrades for shards, an almanac and achievements.
- It needs no network: three.js 0.160.0 comes with the game.
- The game speaks English and Russian, following the language of the app.
