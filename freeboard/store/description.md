Your ship is parked above Vesper. From the aft airlock you can turn into the crew quarters or walk straight down the passage to the bridge. Settle into the pilot seat and watch the harbor traffic cross the windscreen while you choose a contract.

Fly the route yourself. Add thrust, line up a cargo run through the orbital rings or follow a drone on the proximity scope. A survey flight ends on the surface, where you can walk away from the landed ship and collect mineral samples. You can also leave the pilot seat in flight. The ship holds position until you return.

Vesper, Pelagos and Oriole have different markets. The terminal shows the expected return before departure. Spend your earnings on three distinct hulls, interchangeable modules, specialist crew and their berths. Your first mineral sample gets a place aboard. Insurance covers your cargo and equipment.
